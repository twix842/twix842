# Anton Kolotukhin, MD

**Anaesthesiologist | Clinical researcher | Data science**

I am a practising anaesthesiologist with experience in cardiac anaesthesia and intensive care. Alongside clinical work, I conduct research and use statistics and machine learning to study clinical and perioperative data.

## Current work

My current research focuses on haemodynamic changes during induction of anaesthesia and the initiation of mechanical ventilation in patients undergoing coronary artery bypass grafting. I also work on prediction models for perioperative haemodynamic variables.

My main interests are:

- perioperative and cardiovascular physiology;
- clinical prediction models;
- analysis of prospective and retrospective clinical data;
- interpretable machine learning in medicine;
- study design, statistical analysis, and scientific writing.

## Data science

I completed the Yandex Practicum Data Science programme. My project work includes classification, regression, natural language processing, time-series forecasting, bootstrap analysis, and model evaluation.

You can find selected projects in my [Data Science Portfolio](https://github.com/twix842/Yandex_Practicum_DS).

I mainly work with Python, pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, CatBoost, LightGBM, XGBoost, SciPy, statsmodels, Matplotlib, and seaborn.
